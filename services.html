Services

Installation, refinishing & prep

Full-cycle flooring work for residential and light commercial sites — from subfloor readiness through transitions and trim.

Hardwood install & sanding / refinish

New hardwood installation for living rooms, hallways, and open-concept spaces, plus sanding and refinishing when existing floors need a fresh finish rather than a full tear-out.

  • Layout, acclimation guidance, and install to manufacturer practices
  • Sanding and refinishing for worn or tired hardwood
  • Stair and landing coordination where the job requires it
  • Clear protection and cleanup expectations during the visit

Laminate flooring

Click-lock and related laminate systems installed with attention to expansion gaps, underlayment, and clean cuts around cabinets, vents, and doorways.

  • Room-by-room layout planning to reduce awkward end cuts
  • Underlayment suited to the product and subfloor
  • Doorway and closet detailing
  • Residential and light commercial spaces

Vinyl & luxury plank

Luxury vinyl plank (LVP) and related vinyl systems for kitchens, bathrooms (where product-rated), basements, and high-traffic areas that need durability and moisture resistance.

  • Floating and glue-down approaches depending on the product
  • Basement and main-floor installs common in the service area
  • Pattern alignment and transition planning between rooms
  • Advice on product suitability for pets and busy households

Subfloor prep, transitions & trim

Prep is where many flooring jobs succeed or fail. Flooring Doctor focuses on a stable, clean base and finished edges so the new floor looks intentional — not patched in.

  • Subfloor inspection, fastening, and levelling as needed
  • Underlayment and vapour considerations by product type
  • Reducer strips, T-mouldings, and room-to-room transitions
  • Baseboard and quarter-round coordination on install jobs

Site measurement & quoting

Every serious job starts with a visit. We measure, talk through materials and timeline, and provide a quote you can plan around. Scheduling is intentional — Flooring Doctor is not a same-day emergency service.

  • On-site consult for homeowners and property managers
  • Residential and light commercial scopes
  • Material coordination discussion (supply vs. install-only)
  • Booked install windows once the quote is approved
