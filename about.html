About

Josh Tregear — Flooring Doctor

Owner-operated flooring installation based in Bradford, Ontario, with more than a decade of hands-on install experience across the local market.

A local installer you can reach

Flooring Doctor is Josh’s independent flooring business — not a franchise desk and not a rotating crew you never meet twice. When you call 647-463-7789 or email Yourflooringdoctor@gmail.com, you’re talking with the person who measures the job and installs the floor.

Since 2018, Josh has owned and operated Flooring Doctor from Bradford, handling job intake, site measurement, material coordination, installation, and final walkthroughs for residential and light commercial clients.

What that experience covers

  • Hardwood, laminate, and vinyl / plank systems
  • Subfloor prep, underlayment, transitions, and trim
  • Measuring, layout, cutting, and finishing
  • Customer communication and on-site walkthroughs
  • Quoting, scheduling, and job follow-through

How jobs are run

Work is scheduled deliberately. Flooring Doctor is built for planned consults and booked installs — not same-day panic calls. That keeps quality consistent and timelines honest for homeowners in Bradford, Newmarket, Aurora, Barrie, Holland Landing, East Gwillimbury, and the broader York Region / Simcoe area.

Shop discipline matters too. Earlier manufacturing and repair work at Louisville Ladder reinforced careful hand work, quality checks, and following process — habits that show up on every flooring site.
